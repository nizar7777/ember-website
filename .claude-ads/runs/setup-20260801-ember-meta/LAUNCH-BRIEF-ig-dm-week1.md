## The one thing that will cost you money if you skip it

Your ad account bills in **USD**. Your budget is **30 JOD**.

> **Enter 42, not 30.**

30 JOD ≈ 42 USD at the peg. Typing "30" gives you $30, which is about 21 JOD —
you would underspend by roughly a third and wonder why reach was low.

## Before you start — two prerequisites

1. **Instagram account connected** to the *Ember Collection* business portfolio.
   If it isn't, "Instagram Direct" will not appear as a messaging destination and
   the whole setup stalls at the ad set step.
2. **The post is eligible.** Some organic posts can't be promoted — music in
   Reels audio is the usual culprit. If the latest post is blocked, use the most
   recent one that isn't rather than forcing it.

**Why lifetime and not daily:** a lifetime budget cannot overspend. Daily budgets
can run up to 25% over on any given day. For a fixed 30 JD commitment, lifetime
is the safer instrument.

**Why no interests:** at ~$6/day, stacking interests starves delivery. You also
have zero performance history, so any interest I picked would be a guess dressed
up as strategy. Broad lets Meta find the pocket, and week one's real job is to
produce a baseline number.

### Ad
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Ad setup | **Use existing post** |
| Post | Your latest Instagram post |
| Call to action | **Send Message** |
| Primary text | Leave as the post's own caption |

**Use existing post, don't recreate it.** Promoting the real post carries its
organic likes, comments and shares into the ad as social proof. Building a fresh
ad with the same image starts at zero engagement and looks colder.

## Set up the DM greeting

This is the part most people skip, and it's where the money leaks. Someone taps
through, lands in an empty DM thread, and half of them never type anything.

**Greeting message:**

> أهلاً! شكراً لتواصلك مع Ember 🔥
> شو بدك تشوف؟
>
> Hey! Thanks for messaging Ember 🔥 What are you after?

**Suggested replies** — these give people a one-tap way in instead of a blank box:

- `بدي أطلب` / I want to order
- `شو المقاسات المتوفرة؟` / What sizes do you have?
- `كم سعر التوصيل؟` / How much is delivery?

## After you publish

**Within 24 hours**
- Status reads **Active**, not *In Review* or *Rejected*
- Spend has actually started
- That *"verification may be required soon"* advisory on your account hasn't
  turned into a delivery block

**Day 3**
- Check **cost per conversation** and **frequency**
- Frequency above ~3 on a one-week single-city run means you're re-showing the
  same people the same post. Widen to all Jordan or accept the fatigue.

**Day 7 — the number that matters**
- Total spend, conversations started, and **how many of those conversations
  became real orders**

That last one you have to count by hand. Instagram DM conversations never touch
your website, so the pixel cannot see them. Keep a tally in a notebook or a
sheet — without it you'll know your cost per conversation but not your cost per
customer, and only the second one tells you whether this was worth doing.

## What I am not telling you

I'm not giving you a target cost per conversation. I don't have a defensible
benchmark for Jordanian streetwear Instagram DM ads — anything I quoted would be
a number from a different market, a different objective and a different audience,
dressed up as a target for yours.

So treat week one as the measurement, not the test. What you'll get is arithmetic,
not a promise: $42 divided by whatever your actual cost per conversation turns out
to be. That figure becomes your baseline, and every future campaign gets judged
against it instead of against something I made up.

## If you need to stop it

Pause the campaign in Ads Manager. **Pause, don't delete** — a paused campaign
keeps its data so you can learn from it; a deleted one takes the learning with it.
Pausing stops future delivery only; spend already incurred is gone.

Your original Instagram post is unaffected either way. Promoting a post never
alters the post.
