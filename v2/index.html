01Who we are

Clothes that say where you are from, without explaining it

A jamra is the last coal of a fire that never quite went out. That is the whole idea — the parts of this place that survived, worn by someone in their twenties without feeling like a costume.

We do not design anything a visitor would understand. The generic pattern anyone can read does not get made. A saying you heard from your grandmother does. The person who gets it feels seen — that is the actual product.
